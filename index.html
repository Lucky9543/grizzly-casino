How the peg pyramid works

The board is built as a triangle of staggered pegs, with each lower row one peg wider than the row above it. A ball released from the top has a roughly even chance of moving left or right at every contact point, and that pattern repeats until it reaches the bottom. Because of that, the final pocket is shaped by many small 50/50 decisions rather than by a single large one.

Over time, more balls collect near the middle than near the edges, which is why the payout table favors the outer pockets with larger multipliers. Mathematicians call this a Galton board, and the result is a binomial pattern. For players on grizlybet casino, that simply means common outcomes are smaller while rare edge hits pay more.
